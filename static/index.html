Sambhota Tibetan Schools Curriculum Structure

1. Integrated Bilingual Curriculum (CTA DoE & CBSE)

STSS schools deliver a unique dual curriculum that marries academic excellence in science, mathematics, and social studies under the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) / NCERT framework with deep immersion in Tibetan language, calligraphy, Buddhist dialectics, and Tibetan civilization.

2. Categorization by School Stages

Stage Classes Primary Medium & Focus
Pre-Primary Montessori / KG Tibetan mother-tongue immersion, nursery rhymes, motor skills, cultural stories.
Primary Classes 1 to 5 Basic Tibetan Grammar (Dag-yig), EVS, Basic Math, Hindi, Introduction to English.
Middle School Classes 6 to 8 Sumchupa grammar, NCERT Science & Math, Tibetan History, SEE Learning, Dialectics.
Secondary Classes 9 to 10 CBSE Board Preparation (Mathematics, Science, English, Social Science, Tibetan/Hindi).
Senior Secondary Classes 11 to 12 Science, Arts, and Commerce streams with Advanced Calculus, Tibetan Literature & Philosophy.

3. SEE Learning (Social, Emotional & Ethical Learning)

Developed by Emory University with the inspiration of His Holiness the Dalai Lama, SEE Learning provides educators with comprehensive tools for cultivating emotional hygiene, compassion, and systemic thinking in classrooms.
