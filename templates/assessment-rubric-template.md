# STSS Holistic Assessment Rubric
## སློབ་མའི་སྦྱངས་འབྲས་དང་ནུས་རྩལ་དཔྱད་ཞིབ་ཚད་གཞིའི་རེའུ་མིག

This rubric template can be customized for project-based learning, research papers, oral presentations, or dialectic debates (རྩོད་པ) across STSS institutions.

---

### Rubric Breakdown (Total: 20 Marks)

| Performance Area | Level 4: Exceeding (5 Marks) | Level 3: Meeting (4 Marks) | Level 2: Approaching (3 Marks) | Level 1: Beginning (1-2 Marks) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Content Mastery & Vocabulary** | In-depth understanding; effortless bilingual terminology usage; accurate references. | Clear comprehension of major ideas; appropriate vocabulary with minor slips. | Basic understanding; relies heavily on textbook phrasing; occasional misuse. | Fragmented knowledge; frequent inaccuracies in definitions and concepts. |
| **Critical Thinking & Reasoning** | Constructs logical, persuasive arguments; connects theory with real-world Tibetan / global issues. | Sound arguments with adequate supporting evidence and examples. | Arguments are surface-level or lack sufficient empirical/textual backing. | Conclusions unsupported by facts; circular reasoning. |
| **Structure & Clarity** | Cohesive flow; crisp organization; polished grammar and presentation format. | Well-organized with clear transitions; generally grammatically sound. | Somewhat disjointed; organizational transitions need refinement. | Disorganized; difficult to follow the central train of thought. |
| **Collaboration & Ethics (SEE Learning)** | Generously supports team members; values diverse perspectives; models humility and respect. | Cooperates constructively; listens attentively to peers. | Contributes when prompted; minimal active listening during peer sharing. | Dominates or disengages; struggles to maintain respectful peer interaction. |
