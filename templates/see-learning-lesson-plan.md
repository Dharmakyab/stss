### 2. Intended Learning Outcomes (བསླབ་གཞིའི་དམིགས་ཡུལ)
By the end of this session, students will:
1. Identify internal body sensations associated with feelings of safety or distress (Resourcing & Tracking).
2. Cultivate empathetic perspective-taking through deep active listening.
3. Express appreciation for peer support and community interconnectedness.

---
### 3. Session Flow & Practices

#### Step 1: Mindful Pause & Resourcing (དྲན་པ་ཉེར་བཞག - 5 mins)
- Gentle posture check: feet flat on floor, relaxed shoulders.
- Tibetan singing bowl tone or 3 conscious deep breaths.
- Invite students to focus on their personal internal resource (safe memory, teacher, guardian, serene place in nature).

#### Step 2: Body Literacy & Tracking Sensations (ཚོར་བའི་བརྡ་ཆད་རྟོགས་པ - 10 mins)
- Distribute sensation emotion sheets.
- Guide students to distinguish between pleasant, unpleasant, and neutral bodily sensations without judgment.

#### Step 3: Experiential Inquiry & Story (གཏམ་རྒྱུད་དང་བགྲོ་གླེང་ - 15 mins)
- Share a real-life situation involving misunderstanding or conflict.
- Pair sharing: "What needs was the other person trying to meet?"
- Practice reflecting back feelings without interrupting: *"What I heard you say is..."*

#### Step 4: Compassionate Action Pledge (བྱམས་བརྩེའི་ལག་ལེན་དམ་བཅའ - 10 mins)
- Students write or draw one actionable act of kindness they will perform today for a classmate, school helper, or family member.

#### Step 5: Dedication & Closing (བསྔོ་སྨོན་དང་མཇུག་བསྡུ - 5 mins)
- Brief silent reflection wishing peace and happiness to all living beings.
