### 4. Instructional Strategy (5E / Active Learning Model)
1. **Engage (5 Mins):** Real-world scenario, curious question, short visual clip or demo.
2. **Explore (10 Mins):** Guided investigation, data collection, or text analysis in pairs.
3. **Explain (15 Mins):** Student synthesis followed by teacher formalization of terminology and principles.
4. **Elaborate (10 Mins):** Applying the concept to an unfamiliar or higher-order problem.
5. **Evaluate (5 Mins):** 1-minute exit ticket checking individual comprehension.
