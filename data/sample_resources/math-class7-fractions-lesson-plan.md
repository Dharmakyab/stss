# STSS Mathematics Lesson Plan
## Subject: Mathematics | Class: 7th | Duration: 40 Minutes
### Topic: Multiplication of Fractions by Visual Area Models

### Teacher: Lobsang Tsering (STSS Mundgod)

---
### 1. Conceptual Hook (5 Mins)
"Tenzin has 3/4 of a flatbread (Balep korkun). He wants to share 1/2 of that portion with his friend Pema. What fraction of the whole bread does Pema get?"
Ask students to fold a square paper into 4 equal strips, shade 3, and then fold horizontally in half.

### 2. Concrete to Representational (15 Mins)
- Multiplying fractions: $\\frac{a}{b} \\times \\frac{c}{d} = \\frac{a \\times c}{b \\times d}$
- Visual representation of overlap area: $3/4 \\times 1/2 = 3/8$.

### 3. Differentiated Exercises (15 Mins)
- **Level 1 (Foundation):** Basic area grid shaded calculations.
- **Level 2 (Standard):** Word problems involving kilometers traveled between camp settlements.
- **Level 3 (Challenge):** Multi-step recipe scaling problems.

### 4. Formative Assessment Exit Slip (5 Mins)
Solve: $2\\frac{1}{3} \\times \\frac{3}{5}$. Show both visual model and numerical computation.
