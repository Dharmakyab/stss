# STSS Class 10 English Writing Guide
## Chapter: Analytical Paragraph Writing based on Charts/Data

### Aim: To train CBSE Board Class 10 candidates to write concise, structured 100-120 word analytical paragraphs.

---
### Key Structure:
1. **Introduction (1-2 sentences):** Paraphrase the question prompt and state what the graph/data displays.
2. **Body Paragraph 1 (Overview & Trends):** Discuss major trends, highest/lowest points, significant increases or declines.
3. **Body Paragraph 2 (Comparisons & Contrasts):** Draw relevant comparisons between categories.
4. **Conclusion (1 sentence):** Synthesize the overall takeaway without introducing new unmentioned points.

### Useful Vocabulary Bank:
- *Upward Trend:* witnessed a sharp spike, climbed steadily, escalated, reached a peak of...
- *Downward Trend:* plunged, dipped significantly, plummeted, saw a downturn...
- *Stability:* remained constant, leveled off, stabilized around...
- *Comparisons:* in stark contrast to, whereas, twice as high as, marginally lower than...

### Sample Prompt & Model Response:
*Prompt:* The chart below displays the preferred extracurricular activities among Tibetan high school students across STSS schools in 2024 (Tibetan Performing Arts: 38%, Football/Sports: 32%, STEM Club: 18%, Debate/Journalism: 12%). Write an analytical paragraph summarizing the findings.
