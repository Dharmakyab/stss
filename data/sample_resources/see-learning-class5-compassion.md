# SEE Learning Lesson Plan: Classroom Harmony
## བཟང་སྤྱོད་དང་སེམས་མྱོང་ཤེས་ཡོན། སེམས་ཁམས་ཞི་འཇགས་དང་བྱམས་སྙིང་རྗེའི་སྦྱོང་བརྡར།

### Target: Class 5 | Duration: 40 Mins | Setting: Circle Seating

---
### 1. Warm-Up Grounding: "My Safe Resource" (༥ སྐར་མ)
- Ring Tibetan singing bowl or chime.
- Guide students: "Place hands on belly, take 3 gentle natural breaths. Bring to mind a person, pet, or place that makes you feel safe and loved."

### 2. Emotion Thermometer: Tracking Intensity (༡༠ སྐར་མ)
- Introduce the Emotional Scale:
  - Blue Zone: Calm, ready to learn, peaceful (ཞི་བདེ).
  - Yellow Zone: Anxious, frustrated, restless (སེམས་ཟིང་བ).
  - Red Zone: Overwhelmed, angry (ཁོང་ཁྲོ).
- Emphasize: *All emotions are natural; what matters is how we respond without harming self or others.*

### 3. Pair Compassion Practice: Mindful Listening (༡༥ སྐར་མ)
- Partner A shares for 2 minutes: "A time when I felt left out or hurt."
- Partner B practices 100% active listening without interrupting or giving unsolicited advice.
- Reverse roles. Debrief: "How did it feel to be heard with kindness?"

### 4. Group Reflection & Dedication (༡༠ སྐར་མ)
Students write one kind deed they pledge to do for a classmate today on the "Kindness Tree Wall".
